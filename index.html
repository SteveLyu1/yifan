About Me

I am an M.S. student in Software Engineering at Dalian University of Technology, expecting to graduate in June 2027.

My research interests lie at the intersection of natural language processing, large language models, and trustworthy AI. I am particularly interested in how language models use linguistic and social context, why their judgments sometimes fail, and how empirical evaluation and structured system design can improve their reliability.

Beyond individual model outputs, I am curious about the relationship between AI and society—how cultural and social information shapes AI behavior, and how AI systems may influence human judgment and interaction.

Research Interests

LLM Behavior & Evaluation. I investigate how contextual information, prompting, and potentially spurious cues influence model judgments. I am interested in controlled experiments, behavioral auditing, failure analysis, and distinguishing genuine capabilities from evaluation artifacts.

Social & Multilingual NLP. I am interested in social reasoning, culturally situated language, multilingual NLP, bias and fairness, and the quality of datasets and annotations used to evaluate language understanding.

Reliable & Structured AI Systems. I explore how structured representations, evidence-grounded reasoning, multi-perspective collaboration, and verification can improve the reliability and auditability of language models and agentic systems.
