Hello, I'm Steve

Curious about why models behave the way they do.

I study how language models use linguistic and social context, why their judgments can be unreliable, and how careful experiments can help distinguish meaningful capabilities from misleading cues.

I am equally interested in using these insights to design more dependable AI systems. Beyond model behavior, I care about the two-way relationship between AI and society.

01 / Research

What interests
me

LLM Behavior & Evaluation

How do prompting, contextual information, and spurious cues influence language-model judgments? I use controlled comparisons and behavioral auditing to investigate failure modes and the limits of what evaluations actually demonstrate.

Social & Multilingual NLP

How do language models handle culture, identity, discourse, and social perspective? I am interested in social reasoning, multilingual fairness, and the quality of datasets and annotations that support these claims.

Reliable & Structured AI Systems

How can empirical insights lead to better systems? I explore structured reasoning, evidence-grounded arbitration, verification, and coordinated LLM workflows.
